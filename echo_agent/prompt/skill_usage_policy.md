# Skills

Skills are specialized instruction modules that provide additional guidance for specific tasks.

When the current task may benefit from specialized instructions provided by a skill, load the relevant skill before proceeding.

Do not assume or fabricate the contents of a skill before it has been loaded.

After loading a skill, read and understand its instructions. Apply the skill instructions that are relevant to the current task.

When skill instructions reference additional files (for example under references/, scripts/, or assets/), read those resources on demand.

A skill may provide task-specific procedures, constraints, or domain-specific guidance.

When multiple skills are loaded, use the relevant instructions from all applicable skills.

Skills provide additional guidance and do not override higher-priority runtime, strategy, or agent instructions.
